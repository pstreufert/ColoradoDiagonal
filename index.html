THE ASK

How much can one bike do?

This route needs a bike that can do it all; haul heavy gear over rough trails on long days, then cruise over pavement and smooth gravel and eat miles the next. And on the river days, it needs to handle being lashed to a packraft and dragged through rapids, a job no bike was really designed for. The bike for this trip is the Flannimal 6.0.
